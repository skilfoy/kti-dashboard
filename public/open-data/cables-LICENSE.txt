Submarine cables and landing stations: derived open dataset
=============================================================

Files:   /open-data/cables.geojson  (GeoJSON FeatureCollection)
         /api/context?type=cables   (the same data as the dashboard's JSON document)

This dataset is derived from OpenStreetMap: ways tagged communication=line with location=underwater, and nodes tagged
telecom=cable_landing_station, read weekly through the Overpass API. Geometry is simplified to a bounded size and split
at the antimeridian; OpenStreetMap user names, user ids and edit metadata are not kept. Coverage is partial: on
2 November 2026 OpenStreetMap held 374 such ways (189 named) and 12 landing stations, far fewer than the cables in
service.

Share-alike: because this is a derived database of OpenStreetMap data, it is offered under the ODbL 1.0. If you
publicly use an adapted version of it, you must offer that adapted database under the ODbL as well, and keep the
attribution above. Produced works (maps, charts) need only the attribution.

Some cables carry a "wikidata" record (owner, length, service entry or inception date) from Wikidata, which is CC0
(public domain dedication). It is attached only where the OpenStreetMap name and the Wikidata label match exactly after
case, accents and punctuation are removed; other cables carry none.

Not included: TeleGeography's Submarine Cable Map data, which is licensed and is not used here.

Published by the Kilfoy Threat Intelligence platform (https://intel.kilfoycyber.com/data-sources).
