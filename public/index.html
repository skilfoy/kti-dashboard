Blind spots. Open sources only: no human sources, and every level is inherent threat, with no view of any one organization's assets or controls. Insider activity is barely visible in open reporting, and zero-day exploitation appears only after disclosure. Leak-site counts show the victims who did not pay, so they understate the total and overstate groups that publish aggressively. Theaters without current reporting hold at their baseline and are flagged for collection. Party and adversary-controlled statements are graded D or lower or shown as claims.

Entity graph

Every entry the source network and the advisory feeds collect is read against a gazetteer of ATT&CK groups, software and campaigns, MISP threat actors, Malpedia families, leak-site extortion groups, KEV vendors, products and CVEs, and countries. Two entities named in the same entry are co-mentioned. Co-mention shows association in reporting; attribution comes from the curated relationships (ATT&CK, MISP, KEV), which are listed apart. Matching is deterministic and spends no model calls.
