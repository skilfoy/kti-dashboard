Blind spots. Open sources only: no human sources, and residual risk depends on the asset and control data you connect. Insider activity is barely visible in open reporting, and zero-day exploitation appears only after disclosure. Leak-site counts show the victims who did not pay, so they understate the total and overstate groups that publish aggressively. Theaters without current reporting hold at their baseline and are flagged for collection. Party and adversary-controlled statements are graded D or lower or shown as claims.
